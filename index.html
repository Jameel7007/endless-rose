    Dhikr in an endless prayer hall

    Endless Rose

    1. CountClick anywhere, or press Space, for each dhikr.
    2. WalkEach count carries you one bay down the nave, toward the mihrab.
    3. ArriveAt 33 you stand under the dome as its rose is completed. The next count begins the next phrase.
    4. LookMove the pointer to look around.

    The light is the sun where you are, through the day. Light shows today’s prayer times; Ahead keeps the mihrab on the horizon.
